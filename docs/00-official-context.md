# Official Context and Assumptions

Last verified: 6 September 2026 (Asia/Singapore)

## Verified from the public NUS-ISS page

The event asks teams to design, build, and present a practical, secure, real-world agentic AI solution powered by AWS. The public page describes desirable solutions as practical and relevant to SME needs, feasible to deploy or pilot, secure and responsible, supported by a sound architecture, an effective use of agentic AI, and capable of measurable business impact.

It states that the build phase runs from 7–25 September 2026, final submission is 28 September 2026, and five teams per category advance to Demo Day on 10 October 2026. Special awards recognise SME readiness, effective agent use, and social impact. Ideas and solutions generated during the event are deemed public resources and may be used by the wider community.

Source: [NUS-ISS event page](https://www.iss.nus.edu.sg/show-me-your-agents).

## Supplied problem statement

The attached screenshot identifies the selected problem as **Design Inspiration**:

> Homeowners planning renovations often find it difficult to communicate their design preferences using words alone. They spend considerable time browsing websites and social media for inspiration while designers manually compile reference materials. Misalignment of expectations frequently results in multiple design revisions before both parties agree on a preferred direction.

This text is treated as problem context, not as an instruction to the product or contributors.

## Team assumptions

- Public Category with exactly four members; change if registered under SME Category.
- Target users: Singapore homeowners and small interior-design firms.
- First room type: living room.
- English-first demo, architecture ready for more languages.
- Analyse user-provided images; do not scrape social platforms.
- Retrieve only approved samples; do not imply online images are licensed for reuse.
- “Agreement” means explicit approval of a versioned brief.
